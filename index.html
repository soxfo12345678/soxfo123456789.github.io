Academic Projects & Research Highlights

Physiological Lipid Ratio Modeling & Skin Barrier Moisturizer Formulation

Focus: Formulations, Stoichiometry, & Applied Chemistry

Conducted extensive scientific literature research on transepidermal water loss (TEWL) and physiological lipid structures to design and synthesize a targeted skin barrier moisturizer. Addressed ingredient concentration constraints by mathematically modeling and balancing varying lipid profiles in raw materials to achieve a precise 3:1:1:1 ratio of ceramides, cholesterol, nonessential fatty acids, and essential fatty acids.

  • Design of Experiments (DoE)
  • Literature Review
  • Ratio Modeling
  • Emulsification

Key Takeaway: Mastered adjusting mathematical formulations when working with variable raw ingredients (e.g., optimizing milk and olive oil fractions for ceramide yield) rather than ideal textbook values.

Trametes versicolor Enzymatic Fuel Cell Design & PEM Longevity Optimization

Focus: Bioelectrochemical Systems & Materials Compatibility

Designed a bioelectrochemical system concept utilizing Trametes versicolor metabolism to break down plastic substrates and produce disassociated protons for electrical energy generation. Identified a critical biological trade-off: the fungal enzymes targeted plastic-based Proton Exchange Membranes (PEM), requiring system operation limits to short-burst applications (such as powering a small vehicle during a race) to prevent structural degradation.

  • Bioelectrochemical Systems
  • Metabolic Pathway Analysis
  • PEM Design
  • Trade-Off Analysis

Key Takeaway: Mastered evaluating materials compatibility and biochemical kinetics in engineered systems, learning to optimize short-duration performance around strict biological constraints.
